What’s in version 2.3

Everything below is part of the system you open with the button above.

Approve or reject

Buttons on every pending report, tick-box bulk actions, a full review history, and teachers notified instantly.

Classes and assignments

The CPD Coordinator registers classes with enrolment and assigns them to teachers, with an optional subject.

Class coverage

The dashboard shows classes and teachers with no report in the chosen week, term or year.

Safe sign-in

Show/Hide password, Caps Lock warning, strength meter, and lock-outs after repeated wrong attempts.

CPD registration and recovery

A CPD Coordinator can register using the school’s exact name (new registrations are approved by an existing CPD Coordinator) and recover a forgotten password with a one-time recovery code.

Reports and records

PDF and CSV exports, an audit log of important actions, and one-click database backups.

Works on any device

Phones, tablets and laptops, once the system is published online over https.

In-app notifications

A bell with a counter tells people when a report needs review, or when a class has been assigned to them.

Getting started

For a new user on a phone or computer.

  1. Open the systemUse the “Open the system” button at the top of this page. Always use the secure https link.
  2. Sign inUse the username and temporary password given to you by the CPD Coordinator, then choose your own password.
  3. Forgot your password?Teachers, Headteacher and Deputy ask the CPD Coordinator for a reset. CPD Coordinators use their recovery code.
